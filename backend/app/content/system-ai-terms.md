# Talking to the System AI

Before you start, please read this. It takes a minute.

## This conversation is not private

**Event staff can read everything you say to the System AI, and everything it
says back.** There is no private mode and no way to delete a message once sent.

We may quote conversations in the write-up we share after the event, covering
what people tried, what worked, and what we all learned. Assume anything you type
here could appear on a slide.

If you would not put it in a work chat, do not put it here.

## What we record

- Your messages and the System AI's replies.
- Which challenge you were looking at, and when.
- Whether one of our safety filters flagged an exchange.

Conversations are kept for the event and a short period afterwards (currently 30
days), then deleted. Records of flagged exchanges are kept longer, because they
are how we review anything that went wrong — they record *that* something was
flagged, never the answer to a challenge.

## Do not put sensitive information here

The System AI runs on a machine we control, and nothing you type leaves our
network. It is still the wrong place for:

- Passwords, API keys or tokens — **including real ones you use at work**.
- Personal data about yourself or anyone else.
- Customer information, or anything about a real security incident.
- Anything covered by a confidentiality obligation.

Challenge flags are fine. That is the game.

## The System AI is a character, and it is part of the game

It plays a rude, self-important dungeon narrator. The contempt is a performance
and it is aimed at everyone equally.

**Some of its challenges are designed to be broken.** Getting it to say something
it was told not to say is, in places, the entire point. That is a puzzle we
built, inside this event, on purpose.

It is also **wrong sometimes**. It can state things with total confidence that
are simply untrue, including about this event. Check anything that matters, and
never treat what it says as advice from the company.

## Please do not

- **Ask it for material about a real, named person** — a colleague, a manager,
  anyone. It is built to refuse, and the request is not welcome regardless of how
  it is framed.
- **Ask it for tools or instructions aimed at real systems** outside this event.
  Everything here is for the challenges in front of you.
- **Use it to harass anyone**, or to produce content you would not say out loud
  at work.

The event's normal expectations of conduct apply here exactly as they do
everywhere else.

## If you are having a hard time

The System AI is instructed to drop the character completely if someone sounds
genuinely distressed, and to point you towards a person.

If something is wrong, please talk to an organiser or someone you trust. Stepping
away from the event is always fine and nobody is keeping score of you.

## Access can be withdrawn

We can switch the System AI off for one person or for everybody, and we will if
it is being misused. Everything else in the event stays open to you either way.

## Questions

Ask any organiser. If you are unsure whether something belongs in this chat, that
is a good sign it does not.
